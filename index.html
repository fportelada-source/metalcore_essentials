Five directions are carrying the genre's name right now, and they barely resemble each other.

And the next branch hasn't been written yet.

Metalcore is not a straight line.

The genre was born from a collision between hardcore punk and metal, and it kept transforming through dozens of scenes, generations, and cross-pollinations. The categories on this page are historical approximations — they overlap, and reasonable people disagree on where one ends and another begins.

Album covers load live from Apple's iTunes catalog when a confident match exists; where it doesn't — mostly smaller and underground releases — the page falls back to a designed reference plate instead of a broken image. Streaming links go to search results where a confirmed direct URL wasn't available. Dates for 2025–2026 releases were checked against label and streaming-platform listings in September 2026.
