2026

Doom didn't die.
It multiplied.

Five directions are carrying the genre's name right now, and some of them share almost nothing but tempo.

And the tempo still hasn't caught up.

Doom metal is not a genre in a hurry.

The genre was born from Black Sabbath slowing blues-rock down until it turned into dread, and it kept finding new ways to be heavy without being fast — through Swedish epic doom, English death-doom, Finnish funeral doom, American sludge and stoner rock, and Pacific Northwest drone. The categories on this page are historical approximations — they overlap, and reasonable people disagree on where one ends and another begins.
